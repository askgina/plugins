Recovery runs under `askgina-grok47-recovery-20260922.service`, using the tested

## Method and limitations

- Model: `xai-oauth/grok-4.7`, using pinned OMP `18.1.21`.
- Evaluator source: `8e67c7cdd6dacabc108ff20ea112a7b985cab2a3`.
- 35 tasks (4 Spot, 18 Perps, 13 Predictions), three repetitions per setting.
- Every slot had one model dispatch with the same 120-second timeout.
  Automatic supervision continued pending work without replacing model attempts.
- Native model and thinking-level records were checked for all 314 graded
  attempts. Client-recorded effort does not attest provider-internal compute.
- Native tool-use grades apply to all tasks. On the three Perps price tasks,
  `perps-price-evidence-v2` is additionally required for a pass. Of 36 graded
  price checks, 20 passed and 16 failed. Two native passes became effective
  failures: repetition 2 of `perps-hip3-price` at High and xHigh.
- Fresh price checks are displayed independently of historical regrade receipts.
  These results do not establish general answer quality or broad model ability.
- Pricing is unknown. Native zero-dollar placeholders are excluded from cost
  comparisons. Timing and token summaries cover graded attempts.
- Private conversations and tool payloads remain on the VM. The public app
  publishes numeric measurements, check outcomes and provenance hashes only.

## Reproducing the public projection

Run the checked-in `tools/project-eval-grok47.py` on the machine retaining the
campaign evidence, with an unused output directory:

```sh
python3 tools/project-eval-grok47.py \
  --source /home/ubuntu/askgina-grok47-20260921 \
  --output /tmp/grok47-public-projection
```

The exporter verifies frozen configuration and evaluator hashes, the complete
420-slot plan, terminal/request/dispatch identities, native/transcript evidence
hashes, model identity, requested thinking levels, and final coverage totals.
It rejects mismatched effective price verdicts and graded execution failures.
Explicit field selection withholds free-form grader details and provider errors.

Only `results.json` and `snapshot.json` are copied into
`apps/evals/src/results/2026-09-22/grok-4.7/`. The results bind the snapshot by
SHA-256; the snapshot binds the exporter and retained evidence inventory.
The public-artifact guard pins both files and rejects added or changed files in
this publication directory. Native evidence is required to independently
recompute these measurements; its hashes alone are not answer-quality evidence.
