## Question

> What actual-client acceptance scenarios prove exact quote binding, owner approval/denial/timeout, stale/superseded quotes and uncertain outcomes?

## Answer

[INFERENCE] Acceptance requires joining five different boundaries: **the exact action displayed → the human's decision → authoritative authorization of that action → submission or pre-submission refusal → independently attributable outcome**. A rendered card, an Execute button, an OAuth connection, a successful tool response, and an external receipt each establish different things. None substitutes for the entire chain.

[PUBLIC CODE @ 486bb0cbfa78200f8689cd7fbd6bf6d8b9d4104b] Spot documents `intentId`, `quoteId`, and `planHash`, execution of the exact returned quote, supersession/expiry, and reconciliation without automatic resend. These are declared product contracts, not observed enforcement. [S1]

[PUBLIC DOC] MCP Apps provides host-mediated widget tool calls and explicit model-context updates; ChatGPT's tool-result contract makes `structuredContent` and `content` model-visible. OpenAI documents conditional confirmation-gated inputs, annotation hints and server-side authorization requirements. Neither reviewed contract supplies a financial quote-specific signed approval receipt, documented numeric human timeout, exactly-once send guarantee or cancellation rollback guarantee. Host bridge events, widget state and UI closure do not establish those missing server/financial properties. [S4–S9, S15]

[MISSING PUBLIC PROOF] The inspected public setup docs and absent client exercise do not establish the proposed ChatGPT Spot write connection, quote/Execute/approval surface, deployed quote-binding enforcement, rejection/replay semantics, complete submission observation or financial outcome evidence. The pinned ChatGPT guide configures Gina Read; generic ChatGPT MCP write capability does not prove the Gina Spot route. Existing LP list/detail captures are presentation evidence only. [S1, S3]

### Platform-policy prerequisite

[PUBLIC DOC] OpenAI's current guidelines state: **“These guidelines cover all plugins listed in the ChatGPT Directory, including MCP and skills-only plugins.”** Their Overview describes the minimum standard for published plugins in the universal directory shared by ChatGPT and Codex, and also says builders are expected to review and follow the guidelines. The prohibited-services list includes **“Execution of money transfers, crypto transfers, or investment trades”**; its governing sentence forbids selling, promoting, facilitating or meaningfully enabling the listed services. Current commerce is limited to physical goods. [S10]

**[UNRESOLVED] Applicability to the proposed developer-mode/custom-MCP LP workflow versus listed distribution.** The stated Directory scope is not a blanket finding that every ChatGPT/custom-MCP financial workflow is prohibited, nor an affirmative exemption for private developer-mode connections. [INFERENCE / recommendation] Widget API availability is not platform eligibility. Before any funded ChatGPT LP trial, obtain clarification for the exact LP actions and distribution/client context. External handoff is not an inferred loophole: facilitation/meaningful enablement is also within the quoted policy wording. Owner approval cannot waive platform eligibility. Read-only presentation acceptance is separate and cannot establish funded approval acceptance. The owner-confirmed destination remains intact; this report neither resolves platform applicability nor authorizes a run. [S10, S14]

## Recommendations — proposed acceptance within owner-confirmed planning boundaries

### Common scenario-to-evidence bundle

[INFERENCE] Each scenario below needs a private, attributable bundle. These are proposed observation categories, **not new SDK callbacks, existing application fields, or a logging implementation**:

- **C — Client context:** exact client surface/version or web build/date, desktop/mobile context, negotiated protocol/capabilities, host-visible tool schemas/visibility/annotations and connection/access provenance. Private account/context identity must be bound without publishing it.
- **Q — Reviewed quote:** complete authoritative quote snapshot, `intentId`/`quoteId`/`planHash`, action, account/wallet, chain, pool/position/token identifiers, amounts, range/fee settings, minima/slippage, costs/funding bounds, expiration deadline or slot, quote version and relevant revision. A credible pre-send refusal requires evidence that the authoritative gate preceded every possible send for that attempt.

Declare coverage, clock uncertainty and the owner-approved observation/finality rule. Distinguish never submitted, submitted/reverted with costs, pending, partial, unknown and settled result.

Missing any required boundary is a gap, not a pass. If the public client exposes no decision callback, attended screen/interaction observation may establish H; it cannot supply A/O. If an independent trusted A/O channel cannot be obtained, do not claim no-send, replay safety or settlement.

### What can establish no unauthorized send?

[INFERENCE] Separate **attempt prevention** from **outcome absence**:

- A credible, attributable pre-submission gate refusal plus complete coverage of all possible submission routes can establish that a particular refused attempt never crossed the send boundary. It must cover the actual actor, quote and interval, not just a widget flag.
- Trusted attempt/submission-channel observations plus independent external reconciliation can support a bounded claim such as “no submission attributable to this denied quote was observed through the approved window, with the following sources and coverage.” Unavailable/incomplete coverage remains inconclusive or unavailable under the inherited precedence; absence from a single indexer is not definitive.
- Chain/position reconciliation alone can establish observed state/outcomes, not that no unauthorized attempt occurred. No on-chain effect might mean pre-send refusal, a still-pending/unindexed submission, a reverted transaction with fees, or an unobserved route. Status tools and their complete pagination contribute evidence but cannot replace independent provenance.
- A screenshot, spinner, no click, null input, tool cancellation/error, closed iframe, missing result/receipt, model narration, successful outer shell/MCP response or unchanged displayed balances proves neither universal no-send nor settlement. [S1, S5, S8, S9, S12]

[INFERENCE] For settled success, join the **executed plan**, attributable receipt/signature/status/events and post-position/asset changes under an approved chain-specific observation/finality policy. Keep submission, execution failure/revert, partial completion and final settlement distinct. Unrelated transactions, market-price changes, fees and indexer lag can confound balance-only evidence. Any uncertainty freezes new writes; only authoritative resolution plus renewed explicit authority can make a distinct new attempt eligible. No automatic retry or generic model error recovery overrides this rule. Missed cleanup deadlines require owner recovery and explicit resumption approval; an unsafe write attempt stops the entire campaign globally. [S12, S14]

### Human confirmation wait: event-based measurement

[INFERENCE / proposed measurement contract] These event names describe observations, not promised SDK callbacks:

| Event                                                                        | Meaning                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `decision_surface_ready`                                                     | Complete current quote and terms are visible, readable and actionable; progress is genuinely waiting for the owner. Include quote/version/gate linkage. |
| `human_decision`                                                             | Attended observed approve or deny interaction on that exact surface, not server receipt of the choice or tool completion.                               |
| `host_confirmation_visible` / `host_human_decision`                          | Separate observations if ChatGPT presents an additional host gate. Do not assume the bridge exposes these callbacks.                                    |
| `decision_deadline` / `quote_invalidated` / `superseded`                     | Approved deadline or authoritative invalidation ends the pending decision opportunity; absence of choice is recorded as no-decision/censored.           |
| `surface_unavailable` / `surface_ready_again`                                | Coverage or technical availability changes; do not count invisible/disabled technical refresh time as measurable human waiting.                         |
| `outbound_attempt` / `tool_result_observed` / `independent_outcome_observed` | Separate technical submission/result/reconciliation events, not human wait.                                                                             |

Human wait is the **duration of the union of observed intervals where a required decision surface is visible/enabled and progress awaits the human**. For each gate/quote episode $i$, start $s_i$ is observed `decision_surface_ready` (or `host_confirmation_visible` / `surface_ready_again`); end $e_i$ is the first observed decision, approved deadline, authoritative invalidation/supersession, or loss of visibility/coverage. Count only observed available portions: $I_i=[s_i,e_i)$ and

$$T_{\mathrm{human,observed}}=\mu\!\left(\bigcup_{i\in\mathrm{observed}} I_i\right).$$

Here $\mu$ is elapsed duration on the disclosed observer clock. Sequential widget/host gates add; overlapping intervals count once, not once per gate. After a gap, a newly observed ready surface starts another interval; unseen time is unmeasured, not zero or interpolated. A new quote starts a new decision episode. Record component-review and host-confirmation intervals separately before their union, preserving start/end events, quote/gate linkage and termination reason. Coverage-loss truncation and no-decision deadline/invalidation are reported as censoring, not completed human choices. This is a proposed measurement contract; S14 requires separate human-wait measurement but supplies no callbacks or values.

- Approval/denial wait ends at the observed owner interaction. Authorization acceptance/rejection and financial outcome are separate fields; clicking approve on expired Q does not make it valid.
- No-response timeout is right-censored at the approved deadline/invalidation, not called approval or denial. Report completed approve waits, completed deny waits, timeout/censor counts and coverage separately.
- Quote generation, rendering/loading, requesting a host prompt, model/tool computation, network transit and read-only settlement observation are not human confirmation wait. Do not infer human latency by subtracting estimated network time from promise/end-to-end duration.
- A transport/tool timeout after approval is technical uncertainty. Human wait is already complete; no clock event automatically revokes or settles the ambiguous send. Closing a widget cannot retroactively turn approval into no-send.
- Use a common monotonic observer clock when possible; otherwise disclose timestamp provenance, synchronization/error and missing intervals. If a host prompt cannot be observed, host wait is **unmeasured**, not zero. Avoid precision unsupported by attended evidence.
- Keep raw dispatch-to-completion and campaign wall time. Report measured human wait separately from model/tool/network/reconciliation latency; do not silently extend inherited task deadlines or subtract human wait to manufacture a pass. Human confirmation behavior and owner delay must not affect OMP agent-efficiency rankings. OMP agent-task scoring and ChatGPT human-client acceptance remain separate. [S5, S8, S12, S13, S14]
