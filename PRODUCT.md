# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Gina developers and evaluation reviewers inspecting model runs, tool-use conformance, execution coverage, and retained evidence. They need to understand what was actually measured before comparing configurations or deciding what to run next.

## Product Purpose

The evals dashboard makes recorded evaluation results inspectable and auditable. Reviewers can move from campaign coverage to a model, configuration, task family, and its public evidence. A successful review distinguishes completed execution from completed grading and understands the limits of the comparison.

## Brand Personality

Factual, transparent, auditable. Preserve the existing interface and visual design rather than turning a correctness update into a redesign.

## Anti-references

Avoid winner-first benchmark marketing, unexplained aggregate scores, and rankings that disguise missing evidence. Do not make quota failures, timeouts, or other unscored starts look like failed conformance grades. Do not present conditional graded-only rates as comparable headline quality scores.

## Design Principles

- Evidence before conclusions: show coverage, configuration, route, and limitations alongside results.
- Keep execution, grading, and conformance outcomes distinct. Finished runs may show explicitly labeled provisional graded-only percentages alongside coverage. Sort tables by displayed values, including provisional percentages; keep ungraded exclusions visible and retain full-grading requirements for comparison eligibility and efficiency charts.
- Preserve recorded history. Latest means latest recorded, not the most favorable result.
- Keep uncertainty visible. Unknown pricing, missing evidence, and incomplete publication must not become invented values.
- Respect the public/private boundary. Public projections remain sanitized; private visible-chat evidence is separate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard access, meaningful control labels, semantic tables, and screen-reader context. Explain unavailable rates and comparison eligibility in text, not color alone. Retain the current responsive behavior and avoid adding motion or new visual patterns for this correction.
