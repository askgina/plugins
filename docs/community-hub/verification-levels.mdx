---
title: "Verification Levels"
description: "How trust is signaled in Community Hub"
---

Community Hub uses verification tiers so users can quickly assess confidence.

## Levels

### `unverified`

- Entry is published and useful, but not fully maintainer-validated.
- Good for discovery and experimentation.

### `verified`

- Entry has passed maintainer validation.
- Includes a recent verification date.
- Better default for production-like usage.

## Promotion criteria

- Submission has complete capability contract.
- Setup path is reproducible.
- Safety/permission model is explicit.
- Maintainer validation is documented.

## Downgrade behavior

- Stale or broken entries may be downgraded from `verified` to `unverified`.
- Entries are typically downgraded before archival/removal.
