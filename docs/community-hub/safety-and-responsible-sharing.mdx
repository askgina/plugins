---
title: "Safety and Responsible Sharing"
description: "Security, legal, and abuse expectations for community submissions"
---

Community submissions must be safe, attributable, and honest about behavior.

## Security requirements

- Never include secret values (keys, tokens, private credentials).
- List required secrets by name only.
- Declare financial and filesystem side effects explicitly.
- Use least-privilege permission descriptions.

## Legal and attribution requirements

- Submit only content you have the right to publish.
- Include attribution for third-party code or copied snippets.
- Provide clear license context for linked assets.

## Abuse and quality guardrails

- No spam or deceptive claims.
- No malicious instructions or payload references.
- No harassment in discussions/reviews.

## Enforcement

- Submissions may be flagged for changes, rejected, or restricted.
- Repeated violations can result in temporary or permanent publishing restrictions.

## Report issues

If you see unsafe or abusive content, open an abuse report in
`askgina/awesome-gina` using the `report-abuse.yml` issue template and include
the PR/submission link.
