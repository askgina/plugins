Predictions MCP connects supported Model Context Protocol clients to Ask Gina's Polymarket tools. Use natural-language prompts to discover markets, inspect market details and pricing, prepare trades, and review prediction-market positions through your connected client.

For Ask Gina's broader user-facing capabilities, including wallets, market research, Recipes, Memory, networks, fees, and safety boundaries, see the [Product Guide](/product-guide).

## How It Works

The server exposes one MCP tool, `bash`. Your client invokes it from your prompt; inside that sandbox, `host-tools` commands provide prediction-market discovery, pricing, trading, and portfolio operations.
