# Wallet and account

Gina creates a self-custodial EVM wallet and a separate Solana wallet when you sign in. Use each address only on its supported networks.

## Inspect balances

Open the wallet area in the sidebar. **Spot**, **Perps**, and **Predictions** are separate account views; funds in one are not necessarily available in another.

![Wallet overview with total, Spot, Perps, and Predictions balances.](/images/product/wallet-balance.png)

_Actual wallet component with illustrative Storybook balances._

## Find a receive address

1. Sign in and open the wallet area.
2. Choose **Fund**, or ask **“What is my address to receive USDC on Base?”**
3. Match the address to your wallet and confirm the network before depositing.
4. Refresh balances afterward. An in-flight deposit may not appear immediately.

Ask **“Show my linked wallets”** for addresses or **“What do I hold across chains?”** for holdings. Missing data is not a zero balance.

## Export a key

Open wallet settings and choose **Export Privy Wallet**. The provider handles export; never paste the key into chat or support messages. The public Docs Agent cannot access your account.
