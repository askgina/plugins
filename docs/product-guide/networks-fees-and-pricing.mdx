# Credits and network fees

Every new Gina user receives **3 million free credits each month**. Credits pay for using Gina.

## Purchase credits

You can purchase additional credits using **any cryptoasset in your Gina wallet**. Sign in to your Gina account to purchase credits.

## Sponsored network fees

**Network fees are sponsored once you have purchased credits on Gina.** Network fees pay for blockchain transactions; sponsorship is separate from any trading or other fees shown in a quote.

Before a transaction, review the asset, network, amount, route, and quoted fees. After execution, check the transaction result for the final status and reported fees.
