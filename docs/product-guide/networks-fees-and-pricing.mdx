# Credits and network fees

Every new Gina user receives **3 million free credits each month**. Credits pay for using Gina.

## Purchase credits

You can purchase additional credits using **any cryptoasset in your Gina wallet**. Sign in to your Gina account to purchase credits.

![Buy credits screen showing the amount, payment token, estimated spend, Auto top-up switch, Buy button, and purchase history.](/images/product/credits-purchase.png)

_Purchase review captured September 7, 2026. Prices, estimates, and balances are shown as captured. Review Auto top-up before buying; it is enabled in this example._

## Sponsored network fees

Purchased credits can make supported transactions eligible for network-fee sponsorship; check the quote. Wallet Yield requests sponsored EVM exits, subject to availability. Solana LP actions pay network fees from wallet SOL. [LP fees and exits](/product-guide/liquidity-positions) explain the differences.

Before a transaction, review the asset, network, amount, route, and quoted fees. After execution, check the transaction result for the final status and reported fees.
