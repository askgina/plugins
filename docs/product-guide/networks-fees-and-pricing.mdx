# Networks, Fees & Pricing

## Purpose

Network gas pays blockchain validators. Product credits pay for using Ask Gina. They do not cover onchain gas.

## Suitable Use Cases

Check route support, credits, or fee and sponsorship details after execution.

## Prerequisites and Authentication

Sign in to check credits or request a route. Name the asset, network, and destination.

## Inputs and Expected Results

Provide the token, amount, destination, and network. Say whether you mean product credits or gas. Gina returns available route or quote details. After execution, the result reports gas paid and sponsorship when available.

## Tested Example

> Did Gina sponsor gas for the transaction that just finished?

Expected: Gina reports sponsorship only when the execution result includes it.

## Limitations and Safety

Quotes and routes can change. Use the transaction result to confirm gas paid or sponsorship. A draft may omit both.
