# Market Research

## Purpose

Research prices and charts with source links. Start with one asset and metric, such as ETH's seven-day price history. A token price is different from a Polymarket 15-minute up-or-down market.

## Suitable Use Cases

Compare assets, read charts, or verify a market claim.

## Prerequisites and Authentication

Lookups are public. Sign in to save research. Name the network when a token exists on several. If that still leaves tokens with the same symbol, give the contract address.

## Inputs and Expected Results

Provide the asset, metric, time range, and any source. Gina returns available data with links.

## Tested Example

> Show ETH price movement over the last seven days.

Expected: available price history and a factual summary with sources. Not a forecast.

## Limitations and Safety

Feeds can be late, thin, or wrong. Trending is not a safety rating. Verify the contract and source. Past prices do not predict future prices.
