---
title: "Schedule and monitor a recipe"
description: "Choose a cadence, confirm its timezone, and inspect completed runs."
---

First [create and test a recipe](/product-guide/automations/recipes). Scheduling availability depends on your account.

1. Choose **Schedule** on the workflow.
2. Select a suggested cadence or enter a custom one, such as **“Weekdays at 9 AM Europe/London.”**
3. Check the timezone and intended frequency.
4. Use **Schedule workflow now** or **Apply custom cadence**, as shown by the panel.
5. Review the resulting schedule, then open **Automations** to find the configured work and its runs.

Inspect a run's steps, output, and final status. A queued or accepted run has not necessarily completed. For a market report, verify that the result covers the expected assets and reporting period.

If a run fails, read its error and check the inputs, account access, and available credits. Correct the recipe and test it again. Scheduled work can run late or fail because of provider availability, limits, or timeouts.
