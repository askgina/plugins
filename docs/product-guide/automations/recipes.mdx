---
title: "Create and test a recipe"
description: "Turn a task into a reusable workflow and inspect its first result."
---

A recipe describes work Gina can repeat. Start with a research task so you can inspect a useful result without executing a trade. Creation availability depends on your account.

1. Choose **Create** in the sidebar.
2. Enter **“Summarize BTC and ETH market changes with sources. Include the time range and do not trade.”**
3. Review the generated workflow and its inputs. Check that its steps match the request.
4. Keep **Simulated mode** enabled and choose **Run**.
5. Read the step statuses and final output. Confirm both assets, the time range, and sources appear; correct failed or unexpected steps before continuing.

Simulated runs can use live data while simulating side effects. **Run Live** can perform real actions. Review a recipe again after changing its instructions or inputs.

When the result meets your needs, [add a schedule](/product-guide/automations/schedules). If the recipe needs saved research, name the file in [account Memory](/product-guide/memory).
