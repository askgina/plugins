# Automations

Automations let Gina repeat a task without a new chat request each time. A recipe defines the work; a schedule or supported webhook determines when it runs. Creation and scheduling availability depend on your account.

- [Recipes](/product-guide/automations/recipes): build and test a reusable task.
- [Schedules](/product-guide/automations/schedules): choose a cadence and monitor runs.
- [Webhooks](/product-guide/automations/webhooks): respond to supported events and inspect triggers.
- [Copy trading](/product-guide/automations/copy-trading): review a trader and configure supported automated execution.

## Create and test

1. Choose **Create** in the sidebar.
2. Ask **“Each weekday at 9 AM Europe/London, summarize BTC and ETH market changes with sources. Do not trade.”**
3. Review the workflow and inputs. Confirm it performs only the intended research.
4. Keep **Simulated mode** enabled and choose **Run**.
5. Inspect step statuses and output; correct failures before scheduling.

## Schedule and monitor

Choose **Schedule**, select a suggested cadence or enter a custom one, and check its time zone. The panel uses **Schedule workflow now** or **Apply custom cadence**. Review the resulting schedule. Open **Automations** to inspect configured work and runs.

Accepted can mean queued, not completed. Runs may be late or fail because of data, limits, or timeouts.

## Recipes and webhooks

Use the recipe's own trigger configuration for supported webhooks. Its event, signature, and payload must match; there is no universal payload. Never put secrets in prompts or payloads. Avoid relying on a run when failure could cause harm.

Gina Read only inspects schedules; changes require [write access](/write-access/index). Account [Memory](/product-guide/memory) differs from MCP `/workspace`.
