## Explore a market

1. Choose **Perps** in the app sidebar.
2. Search for the asset. Check the venue as well as the symbol for HIP-3 markets.
3. Inspect the market's price, change, volume, and funding information.
4. Ask chat **“Explain the BTC perpetual market and its current funding; do not open a position.”**

## Review your account

Open the Perps account view in the wallet or ask **“Show my Hyperliquid positions and open orders.”** Compare position size, margin, leverage, entry price, and unrealized P&L. Check the result's account and timestamp.

## Understand execution

Opening or closing positions, changing leverage, and funding the account are execution actions. Be explicit about the asset, side, size, and intended action. To research only, say **“do not trade.”**

Perpetual futures use leverage and can liquidate. Read the returned margin and liquidation information before acting. A submitted order is not necessarily filled.
