## Find a market

1. Choose **Predictions** in the app sidebar.
2. Use search or a category to find an event. You can also ask chat **“Find prediction markets expiring this week.”**
3. Open an event and inspect its outcomes, resolution rules, close time, and available prices. Similar titles can describe different markets.
4. Ask Gina to explain a market before choosing an outcome.

Prices and available markets change. A displayed probability is market pricing, not a guarantee of the outcome.

## Inspect positions and orders

Open the Predictions account view in the wallet, or ask **“Show my Polymarket positions and open orders.”** Distinguish an open order from a filled position. Check the market and outcome, not just the event title.

## Review before trading

Use a preparation-only request when you want to inspect terms: **“Explain the cost and outcome of buying Yes in this market; do not trade.”** Give an explicit execution instruction only when you intend to place an order.

For tool-level workflows, see [Predictions capabilities](/predictions-mcp/features). For an external assistant without execution, use [prediction-market research](/read-only/predictions).
