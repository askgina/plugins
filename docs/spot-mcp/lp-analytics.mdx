## Choose the right measurement

| Measurement            | Meaning                                                       |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Current holdings       | Observed inventory with source times and discovery coverage   |
| Estimated fee APR      | A recent fee window annualized at current liquidity           |
| Historical performance | Attributable cashflows, costs and endpoint evidence           |
| Range preview          | A calculation for an explicitly supplied position or scenario |
| Portfolio weights      | A comparison with explicitly supplied spot allocation targets |

Use `getLpPositions` or `getSolanaLpPositions` for current holdings and available fee/APR details. Estimates are not realized returns. The tools below answer historical and hypothetical questions.

The [ChatGPT detail widget example](/spot-mcp/liquidity-positions#position-details-in-chatgpt) also shows **Simulated fee APY**: a hypothetical calculation assuming daily reinvestment at a constant rate. It does not establish automatic compounding or future returns.

## Preview a concentrated position

`previewConcentratedPosition` covers Uniswap v3/v4 on Robinhood Chain at the reviewed application revision. Supply an owned position identity, or an explicit pool, tick range and raw token budgets for a hypothetical position. Do not infer Raydium preview support from the presence of Raydium inventory tools.

The preview reports liquidity, rounded token requirements, unused budget, current and boundary prices, and composition at the current price and range boundaries. Ticks must match the pool's tick spacing. A budget too small for one unit of liquidity can produce zero with a reported minimum usable budget. Optional USD marks retain their own evidence.

The tool does not choose a range, store an executable quote, mint liquidity or promise a fill. An owned-position input describes existing exposure; a hypothetical input describes the supplied scenario.

## Read historical performance

`getLpPerformance` reads a bounded interval for Uniswap v3/v4 on Robinhood Chain or Raydium CPMM/CLMM on Solana. It uses acquired history and endpoint evidence to attribute contributions, withdrawals, collections, rewards and costs, and to calculate available performance measures and a matched holding baseline.

Preserve source times, the observation window and coverage. A current balance cannot establish historical inventory; Solana's `minContextSlot` is a lower bound on a live read, not a historical snapshot. Externally opened, transferred or closed positions require acquired evidence too.

Missing prices, costs, endpoint evidence or fee/principal attribution remain unknown. Do not fill gaps with zero. A stored owed balance can include withdrawn principal; stored fee checkpoints can omit pending growth. CPMM fees remain in the pool value. Pool APR is not a forecast for the position, and historical profit does not establish future returns.

## Separate portfolio calculations

`previewWeightRebalance` compares spot holdings with explicitly supplied target weights. It does not execute swaps or supply LP funding. Settle any separately authorized preparatory swaps before requesting a fresh [LP quote](/spot-mcp/liquidity-positions#fund-and-quote).

The user or external client chooses the pool, capital, range and intended action. These analytics tools do not run a hosted LP strategy, select an automatic allocation, score a guaranteed net edge or establish profitable recentering. Missing transition costs or future economics cannot support a profitability conclusion.
