---
title: "Spot research"
description: "Retrieve token prices, metadata, charts, and completed swap history."
---

Ask **“Use Gina to chart ETH in USD over the past seven days.”** For ambiguous tokens, include the chain and contract address rather than relying on a ticker alone.

The `research-spot-tokens` skill covers live prices, metadata, chart history, and completed swap history. A chart should identify its asset, quote currency, and time range. A price estimate is not an executable quote.

Ask **“Show my completed swaps from the past week”** for account history. Read-only tools cannot prepare or execute a swap or transfer.

Start with [Gina Read](/mcp-access/gina-read) and [connect your assistant](/connect/index). See [Plugins and skills](/agents/plugins-and-skills) for the canonical instructions.
