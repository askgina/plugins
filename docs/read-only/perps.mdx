---
title: "Perps research"
description: "Inspect Hyperliquid and HIP-3 markets, positions, orders, and fills."
---

Ask **“Use Gina to show my Hyperliquid positions”** or **“Compare BTC and ETH perpetual markets.”** Specify the market and venue, especially for HIP-3 assets that can share ticker-like names.

The `research-hyperliquid` skill covers market data, positions, orders, and fills. Distinguish position size, entry price, unrealized P&L, and margin information. Missing data does not mean the account has no position.

![Gina in Claude responding to a HYPE chart request with a Hyperliquid HYPE/USD candlestick chart, interval controls, volume bars, and a text summary.](/images/product/claude-perps-chart.png)

_Gina's MCP chart widget in Claude, captured September 7, 2026. The displayed prices are historical; this example shows a market-data request._

Research access cannot open or close a position, change leverage, cancel orders, or fund an account. Use [Perps write access](/perps-mcp/index) for execution.

Start with [Gina Read](/mcp-access/gina-read) and [connect your assistant](/connect/index). See [Plugins and skills](/agents/plugins-and-skills) for the canonical instructions.
