title: "Prediction-market research"
description: "Discover Polymarket markets and inspect outcomes, books, and positions."
---

Ask **“Find prediction markets expiring this week and explain the outcomes.”** Name the event and outcome when asking about a specific market. Similar titles do not establish that two markets have the same resolution rules.

The `research-prediction-markets` skill covers discovery, order books, public market data, and your positions. Check market identity, outcome, resolution date, and returned liquidity or prices before comparing results.

Research access cannot place or cancel orders, redeem positions, or move funds. Use [Predictions write access](/predictions-mcp/index) for execution.

Start with [Gina Read](/mcp-access/gina-read) and [connect your assistant](/connect/index). See [Plugins and skills](/agents/plugins-and-skills) for the canonical instructions.
