## What do you want to do?

### Monitor your onchain portfolio

Use the **read-only Ask Gina plugin** to review linked wallets, holdings across chains, and market positions. Your agent can research prices and inspect your portfolio without placing trades or moving funds. The plugin combines the Gina Read MCP connection with research skills.

[Set up the read-only plugin](/agents/plugins-and-skills), or connect directly to [Gina Read MCP](/mcp-access/gina-read).

### Have your agent trade crypto across chains

Use **Spot MCP** to research tokens, prepare swaps and transfers, and execute transactions on supported chains. Connect this when you want your agent to act on your instructions, from swapping an asset to sending tokens.

[Explore Spot MCP](/spot-mcp/index) or go to [Spot setup](/spot-mcp/client-setup).

### Have your agent trade on prediction markets

Use **Predictions MCP** to discover Polymarket markets, inspect odds and order books, and buy or sell outcome shares. Your agent can also track positions and manage orders through the same connection.

[Explore Predictions MCP](/predictions-mcp/index) or go to [Predictions setup](/predictions-mcp/client-setup).

### Have your agent trade perpetual futures

Use **Perps MCP** to research Hyperliquid and HIP-3 markets, place orders, and manage perpetual-futures positions. Your agent can inspect margin and funding, adjust leverage, and set stop-loss or take-profit orders.

[Explore Perps MCP](/perps-mcp/index) or go to [Perps setup](/perps-mcp/client-setup).

Spot, Predictions, and Perps use separate [write connections (Degen mode)](/write-access/index). Connect the markets you want your agent to trade; the read-only plugin does not grant execution access.

## Availability and verification

These setup routes are checked against the hosts' documentation. Authenticated Gina smoke tests in every host have not yet been completed. A client supporting MCP does not guarantee every Gina result widget is available.

Public Ask Gina marketplace listings for ChatGPT, Claude, and Cursor have not been verified here. Use the concrete direct-connection or source-install routes on these pages; do not interpret a repository manifest as marketplace approval.

Your client must support remote MCP and the authentication method in its setup guide. Access can depend on your client's plan or workspace policy. Follow the selected MCP's setup guide for the permissions it requires.
