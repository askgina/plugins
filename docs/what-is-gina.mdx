Gina is an AI assistant with a wallet. Ask about markets, see what you own, trade supported assets, and turn recurring tasks into automations—all from the [Gina web app](https://askgina.ai).

![Gina Home dashboard showing the PnL calendar, setup quests, automation activity, and wallet balances.](/images/product/dashboard-home.png)

The Home dashboard brings wallet balances, trading activity, setup steps, and automations together.

## What you can do with Gina

### Research markets

Ask **“Compare ETH and SOL over the past seven days. Include prices, volume, and sources.”** Check the asset, network, time range, and source in the answer. You can explore spot tokens, prediction markets, and perpetual futures.

Continue with [market research](/product-guide/market-research), [Predictions](/product-guide/predictions), or [Perps](/product-guide/perps).

### Understand your wallet

Ask **“Summarize my balances and open positions. Do not trade.”** Gina can help you review supported account holdings and activity. Sign in to access your account data.

See [wallet and account](/product-guide/wallet-and-account) and [transactions and portfolio](/product-guide/transactions-and-portfolio).

### Prepare a transaction

Ask **“Prepare a quote to swap 10 USDC to ETH. Do not execute.”** Check the network, asset addresses, amount, route, and fees before requesting execution. A direct send or swap instruction can authorize an actual transaction; use explicit proposal-only wording while exploring.

The web app supports execution. External assistants need the appropriate [write connection](/write-access/index); the research plugin is read-only.

### Automate recurring work

Create a morning market report, reuse a research recipe, or respond to a supported webhook event. Copy trading has its own setup and execution controls. Start with [Automations](/product-guide/recipes-and-webhooks), test the workflow, and inspect its result before enabling unattended execution.

### Keep context for later

Save a watchlist or research note in account Memory, then ask Gina to read it in a later conversation. [Files and memory](/product-guide/memory) explains how to name the file and check what was saved.

## Start in the web app

Open [Gina](https://askgina.ai) in your browser. No MCP installation is needed for the web app.

### First chat

1. Choose **Log In** or **Launch Gina** and sign in.
2. Choose **Chat** in the sidebar.
3. Ask **“Show ETH's price in USD over the past seven days.”**
4. Check the asset, time range, and source in the result. No deposit is needed.
5. Use **Chats** to return to earlier conversations.

### Next steps

- [Wallet](/product-guide/wallet-and-account): addresses and balances.
- [Transactions](/product-guide/transactions-and-portfolio): holdings, proposals, and execution.
- [Research](/product-guide/market-research), [Predictions](/product-guide/predictions), and [Perps](/product-guide/perps): markets and positions.
- [Automations](/product-guide/recipes-and-webhooks): create, test, and schedule work.
- [Files and memory](/product-guide/memory): reusable research.
- [Agent Setup](/agents/authentication): connect external assistants.

A request to **send** or **swap** can authorize execution. Say **“prepare”**, **“quote”**, or **“do not execute”** to request a proposal only. A chat response does not prove settlement.

Public docs need no account; balances, files, automations, and transactions do. Never paste a private key or recovery phrase into chat. See [support](/product-guide/safety-support-and-limitations) for help.

Every new Gina user receives **3 million free credits each month**. Purchase additional credits using any cryptoasset in your Gina wallet. Network fees are sponsored once you have purchased credits on Gina. See [credits and network fees](/product-guide/networks-fees-and-pricing).

## Use Gina in your assistant

You can also connect Gina to a supported assistant through MCP, which provides tools the assistant can call. A plugin bundles host-specific setup and skills that teach the assistant how to use those tools.

Start with [read-only access](/mcp-access/index) for research and account visibility. Trading and transfers require separate **write access (Degen mode)**. Installing a research skill does not grant execution permissions.
